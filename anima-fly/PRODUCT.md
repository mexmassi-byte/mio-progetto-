# ANIMA FLY: product context

Assumptions are marked (assumed). The owner answered the name and language in this session, and the brief comes from their message.

## What it is
ANIMA FLY is a concept website for a private jet charter brand. It is a showcase built by the owner's studio, not a live operator: nobody can book a real flight on it. Platform: web, desktop first and complete on phones.

## Users
People who fly private or are about to: founders, executives and families who value time over price. They arrive from a social video or a referral, usually on a phone in the evening. On desktop it is often an assistant or a travel manager making the booking. (assumed)

## Purpose
In one scroll the page sells the feeling of flying private: no terminal, your car at the airstair, your cabin, above the weather, landing anywhere by tonight. It ends at one action: request a quote / plan a flight.

## Positioning
Cinematic and calm, never flashy. The film does the persuading and the type stays out of its way. It is the opposite of a broker site with stock grids and price tables.

## Operating context
- The owner supplied the hero film (a jet at sunset on wet tarmac, take-off, above the clouds, an island runway; 12 s, 736 x 400). It drives the scroll-scrubbed hero.
- The owner supplied the reference reel ("POV: Your Website Is As Premium As Your Service") as the spatial and motion reference. The owner's words: "fallo uguale al video che ti ho mandato, più che puoi, magari migliora cose che trovi possibili da migliorare".
- English copy (owner's choice). Brand name ANIMA FLY (owner's choice).

## Constraints
- Concept only: no invented reviews, client names, awards, prices or performance claims presented as fact. Aircraft class figures are typical ranges, labelled as such.
- The reference belongs to another studio: its layout, motion and type pairing are followed, but the copy, brand and mark are our own.
- Every photograph is openly licensed (Pexels License) and credited. The hero film is the owner's.

## Brand commitments
- Dusk aviation: dark ground, a champagne-gold accent, warm white type.
- A heavy wide grotesque for statements, paired with an italic serif line.
- A gold flight path threads the page after the hero: one leg per section, each drawn with the scroll and landing on what you can act on.

## Principles
1. The film leads; text sits where the picture is calm.
2. Every number on screen means something (altitude, heading, time).
3. One action, always reachable: plan a flight.

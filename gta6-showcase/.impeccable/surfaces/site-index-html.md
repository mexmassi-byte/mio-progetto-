# Surface brief: launch fan page (site/index.html)

Scope: the one-page fan site and its record mode for the 9:16 video. Visitor mode: Persuade (hype content, not an ad).
Audience and job: GTA fans on phones on launch week; they should feel the world, learn it is out now and where, meet Leonida and the duo, play the fan film.
Proof and content: Rockstar's published facts only (out now Nov 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; Leonida and its six places; Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos; official films and dates); real credited photographs of Ocean Drive and Miami; royalty-free footage.
Constraints: no Rockstar art, logos or footage; unofficial fan concept stated; no prices; no studio credit.
Memorable moment: the Lights switch, the page goes from pastel day to neon night and every sign lights.
Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Vice City as the Art Deco hotels of Ocean Drive: each section is a facade whose vertical name sign says literally what it holds, and one light switch turns the whole page from pastel day to neon night. It refuses the category default: dark key-art hero, glowing logo, neon gradients, glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat stucco fields in flamingo pink and mint by day, Atlantic navy by night; one neon azure lights the signs and the single action, four inks and nothing else. Big Shoulders Display stacked vertically for the signs, Big Shoulders Text for reading; a lit sign switches to its inline tube cut, never a blurred glow. Real photographs of Ocean Drive and Miami at night, square corners, one window-bay grid.

STORY: The visitor sees that Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, walks past Leonida's places, meets Jason and Lucia in Rockstar's words, reads the official films with their dates, plays the fan film, and leaves for Rockstar's own page.
